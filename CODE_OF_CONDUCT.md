# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Aether Engine to be a welcoming community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socio-economic status.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Respect that people work in different languages and time zones.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or events.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events — and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through a GitHub security advisory marked "conduct" or by contacting any maintainer listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants, in proportion to the harm.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may be removed from their role.
