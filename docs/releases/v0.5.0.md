**Materials, lights and shadows**: physically based rendering, placeable lights and real-time sun shadows.

Built entirely from this repository by GitHub Actions. Early preview.

## Download and run

1. Download `aether-engine-v0.5.0-windows-x64.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Run `aether_editor.exe`. The demo now has a glowing sun that lights the planets, shiny metal moons and shadows on the floor.
3. Open `levels\model_garden.alevel` to see a normal-mapped crate, a polished gold sphere and a warm lantern.
4. Add lights from **Place Actors > Lights**. Select any mesh to change **Metallic**, **Roughness** and **Emissive** in Details; use the **World Settings** tab for sky light, exposure and shadows.

Requires a GPU driver with **Vulkan 1.3**. Binaries are not code-signed yet; verify with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## New

- **Physically based materials** (the glTF metallic-roughness model): base colour, metallic, roughness, normal maps and emission, with filmic (ACES) tone mapping. Models from Blender and other tools now look as their authors intended.
- **Lights as actors:** directional (sun) and point lights you place, move, aim, edit and save with the level. They show as icons in the viewport and can be clicked.
- **Sun shadows** with soft edges, fitted to the area around the camera.
- **Sky light** from above and below; metals reflect it instead of turning black.
- **World Settings** panel: sky colours, ambient brightness, exposure, shadows on/off.
- Older levels open unchanged; a level without lights gets a default sun.

## Quality

- 140 automated tests on GCC, Clang and MSVC, under AddressSanitizer and ThreadSanitizer. New pixel tests check the light direction, emission, metal vs non-metal, normal maps, point-light range and shadows on software Vulkan with validation.

## Known limitations

- One shadow-casting sun; point lights do not cast shadows yet; shadows cover about 40 m around the camera target.
- Up to 16 point lights at a time (nearest to the camera); no reflections of the scene itself (no environment maps yet); no transparency.
- World Settings are not saved in the level yet. Materials are set per object; shared material assets are planned.
- Verified on software Vulkan in CI.
