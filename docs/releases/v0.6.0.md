**Sky, reflections and better shadows**: a real sky behind your scene that also lights it and shows up in reflections, and sharp shadows near and far.

Built entirely from this repository by GitHub Actions. Early preview.

## Download and run

1. Download `aether-engine-v0.6.0-windows-x64.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Run `aether_editor.exe`. The demo level now has a blue sky with the sun in it; the metal moons reflect the sky.
3. Open the **World Settings** tab (next to Details) and change the sky's zenith, horizon and ground colours, its brightness, the exposure, or turn the sky and shadows off. Everything is saved with the level and can be undone.
4. Open `levels\model_garden.alevel` and look at the gold sphere: it mirrors the horizon.

Requires a GPU driver with **Vulkan 1.3**. Binaries are not code-signed yet; verify with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## New

- **Sky:** a gradient sky with the sun's disc, drawn behind the scene.
- **Sky lighting:** the sky lights objects from every direction, and smooth or metallic surfaces reflect it (blurrier as roughness goes up).
- **Cascaded shadows:** three shadow maps at increasing distances, so shadows are detailed close up and still present 80 m away, and stay steady as the camera moves.
- **Environment is part of the level:** sky, exposure and shadow settings are saved in the `.alevel` file. Levels without one get the editor's default sky.

## Quality

- 147 automated tests on GCC, Clang and MSVC, under AddressSanitizer and ThreadSanitizer. New pixel tests cover distant shadows, the sky background, sky lighting and mirror reflections on software Vulkan with validation.

## Known limitations

- Reflections show the sky only, not other objects. The sky is a colour gradient, not a photo (HDR) or a simulated atmosphere; no clouds.
- Point lights still cast no shadows. Where two shadow cascades meet, a slight change in sharpness can be visible.
- No transparency yet. Verified on software Vulkan in CI.
