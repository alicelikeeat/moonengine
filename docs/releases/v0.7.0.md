**Physics**: objects fall, collide, stack and bounce when you press Play.

Built entirely from this repository by GitHub Actions. Early preview.

## Download and run

1. Download `aether-engine-v0.7.0-windows-x64.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Run `aether_editor.exe`, open `levels\physics_playground.alevel` (File > Open Level) and press **Play** (Alt+P): a heavy ball smashes a wall of bricks, balls roll down a ramp, a pyramid of crates stands by. **Stop** (Esc) puts everything back.
3. In any level: *Place Actors > Physics Cube* or *Physics Sphere*, then Play. Or select any object and use *+ Add Component > Rigid Body*.
4. Without the editor: `aether_spinning_cubes.exe --level levels\physics_playground.alevel`.

Requires a GPU driver with **Vulkan 1.3**. Binaries are not code-signed yet; verify with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## New

- **Rigid body physics**, powered by the open-source Jolt Physics library: gravity, collisions, stacking, friction and bounciness.
- **Colliders:** box, sphere and capsule, fitted to the object's mesh with one click and shown as a green wireframe when selected.
- **Body types:** Dynamic (falls and gets pushed), Kinematic (you move it, it pushes others), Static (never moves). A collider on its own is a solid obstacle such as a floor.
- **Editable in Details:** mass, friction, bounciness, gravity scale, damping, starting velocity. Saved with the level.
- **Sphere** as a basic shape, next to the cube.

## Fixed

- Stopping Play removed lights and the sky settings from the level, and changes to them could not be undone (a mistake in 0.5.0 and 0.6.0). Fixed, with a test that keeps future components from being left out.

## Quality

- 162 automated tests on GCC, Clang and MSVC, under AddressSanitizer and ThreadSanitizer. Physics tests check free fall against the formula, resting contact, stacking, bouncing, pushing, raycasts and that a simulation repeats bit for bit.

## Known limitations

- Box, sphere and capsule colliders only (no mesh-shaped colliders yet). No joints, character controller or collision events.
- Physics runs on one CPU thread, at 60 steps per second without smoothing in between.
- Imported models do not get colliders automatically; add them per object.
- Verified on software Vulkan in CI.
